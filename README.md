# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze revenue, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and marketing efficiency.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of key sales metrics while allowing users to explore performance through multiple interactive filters.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform sales data into an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that helps users:

- Track overall sales and profitability
- Monitor revenue and profit trends over time
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Compare profitability across countries and regions
- Analyze marketing spending against profit
- Compare current performance with the previous year
- Explore sales performance using interactive filters

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 1. Net Revenue Over Time

A monthly trend visualization showing changes in net revenue across the selected period.

### 2. Profit Over Time

Tracks monthly profit performance and provides an overview of profitability trends.

### 3. Revenue Share by Category

A donut chart showing the contribution of different product categories to total revenue:

- Electronics
- Clothing
- Home & Kitchen
- Beauty
- Others

### 4. Top 10 Products by Profit

Ranks the top-performing products based on their contribution to total profit.

### 5. Profit by Country / Region

A geographic visualization used to analyze profitability across different countries and regions.

### 6. Marketing Spend vs Profit

A scatter plot comparing marketing expenditure with profit and displaying the correlation between the two variables.

**Displayed Correlation:** 0.68

---

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes multiple slicers for detailed analysis:

- Year
- Quarter
- Month
- Region
- Country
- Product Category
- Brand
- Sales Channel
- Customer Type
- Promotion Type
- Sales Person
- Date Range

These filters allow users to move from an overall business view to detailed segment-level analysis.
